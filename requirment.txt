Do NOT add HowTo markup (deprecated by Google) or AggregateRating/Review (we have no genuine reviews).

404 page has noindex and must be served with a real 404 status (Cloudflare Pages does this with 404.html — verify the export produces it).
All main content and links are present in the static HTML (server-rendered at build). Tool UIs may hydrate on the client, but headings, intro, how-to, content, FAQ and links must be in the initial HTML. Verify by inspecting files in /out.
Every page reachable within 3 clicks from the homepage. No orphan pages. Breadcrumbs on all non-home pages.
Use <a>/next/link for all internal links (no JS-only navigation).
Every image has meaningful alt text, width and height.
